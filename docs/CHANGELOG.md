# Changelog

What's changed in Run Escape, newest first. The game shows this on its title screen too (click
the version in the corner), so it's written for players.

## Custom Edition

### Co-Op Multiplayer & Leaderboards

- **2-4 Player Co-Op Rooms**: Host or join Co-Op lobbies across all maps with synchronized waves, enemies, bosses, revives, overhead chat, per-weapon stances, power-ups (Sled, Giant rune essence, Evil Bob, Fairy ring, Drill Demon), a Host-selectable **Difficulty Multiplier (1x - 3x)**, and automatic **In-Game Lobby Locking** so latecomers cannot join or disrupt an active run.
- **Shared Progression & Casket Rolls**: Co-Op runs share the Host's shop upgrades and pool party XP bonuses. Bones, coins, and Antique Lamps split across the party, while each casket opens once to grant every player their own unique item roll.
- **Party HUD & Leaderboards**: Live party cards with player levels, health, and active prayers, a post-run **Co-Op Run Leaderboard** with individual damage and point scores, and a **Level Points & Best Times Leaderboard** on the Title screen and Map Select panel.
- **Level Top Score System**: Earn a run score on every level based on monsters slain, kill pace (kills/min), bosses defeated, fast boss clear speed, character level, survival time, and endless rounds, scaled by each map's difficulty multiplier (`1.00x` Lumbridge to `3.33x` The Inferno).
- **Challenge Modes (Extreme One Chunk Man & Nightmare Man)**: Choose **Extreme One Chunk Man** (**2x** map difficulty for **1.5x** points) or **Nightmare Man** (**3x** map difficulty for **2.5x** points) on the Map Selection screen or in Co-Op lobbies to test your build against 2x or 3x monster and boss HP and damage for massive leaderboard scores.

### Maps, Bosses, Quests & Adventurers

- **Barbarian Village Campaign**: Unlocked after beating **Lumbridge**. Battle through **Barbarian Village (Gunnarsgrunn)**, the Stronghold of Security, and Edgeville Dungeon against barbarian villagers, axe-throwers, shieldmaidens, berserkers, Ice Mountain wolves, Dwarven Mine scorpions, minotaurs, giant spiders, shades, and ankou. Defeat five campaign bosses across the 15-minute run: **The Minotaur Warlord** (3:45), **Obor** the hill giant boss (7:30), **Haakon the Champion** (9:30), **Bryophyta** the moss giant boss (11:15), and **Chieftain Gunthor the Brave** (15:00).
- **Penance Fighter & Campaign Unlocks**: Beat **Barbarian Village** to unlock the **Penance Fighter** adventurer (clad in a **Fighter torso**, **Berserker helm**, **Penance skirt**, **Penance gloves**, and **Runner boots**, starting with a **Bronze battleaxe**), the **Battleaxe** weapon line (Bronze -> Dragon -> **Leaf-bladed battleaxe**), the **Zombie axe**, the **Fighter torso**, and all four Fremennik helmets: **Berserker helm**, **Warrior helm**, **Archer helm**, and **Seers helm** (`farseer_helm`).
- **Barbarian & Edgeville Boss Weapons**: Slay the bosses of Barbarian Village to unlock **Hill giant club** (Obor), **Bryophyta's staff** (Bryophyta), and **Skull sceptre** (The Minotaur Warlord).
- **Fight Caves & Protection Prayers**: Enter the **Fight Caves** map and use **Protection Prayers (Q / E / R)** to block Magic, Ranged, and Melee boss attacks from TzTok-Jad and TzKal-Zuk.
- **Cook's Assistant Quest (Lumbridge)**: Speak to the Lumbridge Cook and gather an **Egg**, **Bucket of Milk**, and **Pot of Flour** across Lumbridge for bonus XP, coins, and a guaranteed chest.
- **Slayer Tasks & Slayer Masters**: The checklist tab and HUD tracker feature **Slayer Tasks** grouped by OSRS Slayer Masters (**Turael**, **Mazchna**, **Krystilia**, **Sumona**, **Vannaka**, **Duradel**, **Konar quo Maten**, **Chaeldar**, **Aya**, **Nieve**, **Steve**, **TzHaar-Ket-Keh**, and **Spria**).
- **Secret Adventurers & Codes**: Redeem secret codes in **Settings -> Save & Codes** to unlock **Dipper the Corgi**, **Elias the Chihuahua**, and **Nova the Cat** (Neite).

### Combat, Balance & Quality of Life

- **Blood Burst -> Blood Barrage**: Evolve **Blood Burst** into **Blood Barrage** with full multi-target AoE radius matching Ice Burst and Ice Barrage.
- **Rapid Heal**: Rebalanced to **+2.5 HP/min per level** (+12.5 HP/min at level 5); taking a hit pauses Rapid Heal regeneration for **5 seconds**.
- **God Wars Dungeon Faction Warfare**: Rival God Wars armies spot each other from further away (`14` tiles), ranged and magic troops fire projectiles at rival factions, and monsters slain by rival factions drop XP bones.
- **Unreachable Loot Recovery**: Pickups dropped over walls, water, lava, trees, rocks, or impassable terrain by flying or ghost monsters automatically slide onto the nearest walkable tile.
- **Armoury Refund & Settings**: Added a **Refund all** button in the Armoury, **UI Scale** options (**0.5x - 2x** plus **Auto**), a **Music** volume slider in Settings, and a **Tile Coordinates** HUD toggle in Settings.
- **Full OSRS Soundtrack**: Added OSRS music tracks across the Title screen (**Scape Main**) and all 20 campaign and fun maps (**Harmony**, **Barbarianism**, **Dangerous Way**, **Wilderness**, **Arabian**, **Rellekka**, **Jungle Troubles**, **March of the Shayzien**, **Mistrock**, **The Curtain Closes**, **Armageddon**, **Elven Seed**, **Inferno**, **Fire and Brimstone**, **The Trade Parade**, **Adventure**, **Massacre**, **Last Man Standing**, **Melodrama**, and **Null and Void**).
- **Android & iOS Mobile Browser Support**: Added a floating **Virtual Touch Thumbstick** for movement, **two-finger pinch-to-zoom**, **Fullscreen** mode with automatic landscape lock and hardware safe-area inset support, an on-screen **Full / Pause** action bar in the HUD, tap-to-inspect item and slot tooltips, and automatic responsive scaling for mobile screens.
